# Code of Conduct

VisuSic welcomes people with different experience levels and backgrounds.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Give actionable feedback and criticize ideas rather than people.
- Respect privacy, consent, and other people's time.
- Help newcomers understand decisions without gatekeeping.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, and deliberate disruption are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner through GitHub. The owner may remove harmful content and restrict participation when necessary. Decisions should be proportionate and focused on keeping the community safe.
